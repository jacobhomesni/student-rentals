FAQs

Where do Queen’s students usually live?

Most look first at Botanic, the Holylands, Stranmillis, Malone, the Lisburn Road and Ormeau. All are walkable or a short bus from campus.

When should I start looking near Queen’s?

Many groups start looking from late autumn through spring for the next academic year. Popular streets near Queen’s go early, so it helps to search before summer if you want more choice.
